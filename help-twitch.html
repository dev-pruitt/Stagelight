FROM SETUP TO TROUBLESHOOTING

Stagelight for Twitch

Check your starting soon scene before it reaches your viewers.

Start with your encoder

  1. Set up your Stagelight capture in OBS.
  2. Make a local recording and confirm picture and sound.
  3. Use your existing Twitch connection in your streaming software.
  4. Check your Twitch preview and compare it with the recording.

When something goes wrong

OBS looks good, but viewers see something else

Confirm the scene being sent live is your starting soon scene. Check your encoder’s connection and platform preview. A working local recording does not verify the live broadcast.

Music is present live but missing from the VOD

Check your encoder’s live and VOD audio track routing. If Twitch shows a mute notice, review that notice. A separate VOD track and a platform mute are different issues.

Platform login or stream key problem

Stagelight does not handle Twitch login or stream keys. Use Twitch or your encoder’s support for that connection. Never include a stream key in a Stagelight report.

For platform-side guidance: Twitch Help. Stagelight is independent of Twitch.
