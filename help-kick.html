FROM SETUP TO TROUBLESHOOTING

Stagelight for Kick

Build your starting soon scene in your encoder and check the broadcast separately.

Prepare your stream

  1. Set up Stagelight in OBS.
  2. Check a local recording for the timer, music, and framing.
  3. Confirm your Kick connection in your encoder using your Creator Dashboard settings.
  4. Set your stream title and category, then check your channel preview when broadcasting.

Encoder works, but Kick does not

Check the service connection and the platform’s error message. If you reset your stream key, update the encoder too. Keep the key private; Stagelight does not need it.

Audio missing or doubled

Play the local recording first. If the problem is there, check your encoder’s audio sources. If it only happens on the channel, check the outgoing audio settings and platform guidance.

Source: Kick streaming setup. Mobile broadcasting uses a different app and is not covered by these desktop steps.
