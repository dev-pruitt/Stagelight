Stagelight for YouTube Live

Use a test recording and YouTube’s stream preview to narrow down problems.

Before your live stream

  1. Prepare your scene in OBS.
  2. Record a short countdown and listen to the file.
  3. Open YouTube Studio’s Live Control Room for your stream.
  4. Check the incoming preview and stream health before starting your event.

Picture or sound fails

If it fails in the local recording, return to capture and audio setup. If the recording is good, read the errors in Live Control Room and check the encoder’s outgoing connection. Share the error text when reporting a Stagelight-related problem.

Playback seems delayed

Compare the local countdown with the stream preview. Broadcast latency can delay the viewer’s picture. Do not keep changing the timer to chase a delayed preview.

Sources: YouTube live troubleshooting and encoder setup. Stagelight does not manage your YouTube connection.
