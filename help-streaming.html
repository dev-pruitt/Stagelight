Find the broken link

  1. Check Stagelight in your browser.
  2. Make a local recording in your streaming software.
  3. Compare that recording with your platform preview.

If the browser fails, report Stagelight. If only the recording fails, check capture and audio sources. If the recording is good but the broadcast fails, check the platform and encoder connection.

Phone, tablet, or console?

The guides below use a computer with OBS. Mobile and console broadcasting have different capture limits. Stagelight playback while switching mobile apps is not verified. There is no direct Stagelight platform connection or console plugin.
