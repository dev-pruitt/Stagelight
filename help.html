Use it in a meeting

Before people arrive

  1. Open Stagelight. Add your title, logo, and songs.
  2. Choose your countdown. Turn off Full screen if you want to keep meeting controls nearby.
  3. Share the Stagelight window or tab with sound enabled.
  4. Click Begin presentation. Ask one attendee to confirm they can see and hear it.

Use headphones. Close other noisy tabs. Stop sharing before returning to setup.

Make it yours

Countdown

Use the minutes slider for a fixed wait. With songs added, auto-match fits the countdown to the playlist. Turn it off to choose your own duration.

Music & transitions

Audius needs no sign-in. Upload plays your own audio. Both blend songs for up to 3 seconds. Spotify is in beta, for approved accounts with Premium; Stagelight does not crossfade Spotify tracks.

Logo & colors

Upload a logo to pick colors from it. Choose album colors to follow the music, or tap a color swatch to override it.

Design

Search or scroll the design library. Use New, Retro gaming, Streamer, Ambient, or Minimal to narrow it down. Multiple builds a design sequence; changing a category keeps your choices. Shuffle changes the design order. Liquid Glass adjusts the glass effect.

Display font

Choose a font below the design library. It updates the countdown and display text in both preview and presentation. Your font stays saved in this browser.

Full screen

The Full screen button controls whether presentation starts full screen. Press Esc to leave browser full screen.

Saved setup & Reset

Your setup stays in this browser. Uploaded audio must be selected again after reopening. Reset clears the current setup. Undo reset brings it back until the tab reloads. If this browser cannot save, Stagelight shows a warning; keep the tab open until you have saved what you need.

Quick fixes

I hear music, but the meeting does not.

Enable sound in the meeting’s sharing controls. Check the shared tab or window is Stagelight. Confirm with another attendee.

Sound echoes or seems distorted.

Use headphones. Check for a second player, camera monitor, or meeting tab playing the same sound. Pause each source to find the duplicate.

A song will not start.

Try its preview first. For uploads, select the file again. For Spotify, check Premium and app access, then disconnect and reconnect. Try Audius or an upload if that service is unavailable.

The timer looks wrong.

Check auto-match and your playlist length. In auto-match mode, the timer follows playback and may wait while music loads. If it jumps repeatedly, report the source and exact steps below.

The screen is slow.

Try another design and close busy tabs. Test before attendees arrive. Keep the presenting computer awake.

Get a prewritten email to support@t64.org. Add what happened, then send from your own email app.
